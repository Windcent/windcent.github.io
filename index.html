About Me

I am a Master's student in Engineering at the Université du Québec à Rimouski (UQAR), specializing in electrical engineering, data science and system modeling. My research focuses on real-time fuel consumption estimation models for ships, collaborating with OpDAQ Systems. I am passionate about applying technology to solve real-world challenges. My interests span across sciences, technologies, and fabrication, and I actively contribute to academic and technical communities.
